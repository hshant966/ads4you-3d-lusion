03 — How it runs

Five days, in the open.

You are on the call, not waiting on a status email. Each day ends with something running that you can message.

Day 0

Intake call

45 minutes. What you actually do every day, which tools hold your data, which channel you already live in, and the three jobs worth automating. We tell you on the call if you are not a fit.

Day 1–2

Server & channel

Hardened VPS, your accounts, your identity. The agent boots on its own and survives a reboot. You message it the same day.

Day 3–4

Three workflows

We build the jobs you named, test each one against real inputs, and tighten them until they behave the way you expect.

Day 5

Handover

Recorded walkthrough of everything, your handbook, your cost sheet, root credentials handed over, and 14 days of support.

04 — Approval first

Anything that sends, posts, pays, deletes, or publishes runs in draft mode until you say go. Every approval is one tap. Every action is logged.
