01 — The problem

The software is free. The setup is the product.

Hermes and OpenClaw are open source and MIT licensed. Anyone can download them. Almost nobody can finish wiring them: servers, channels, secrets, schedulers, and the updates that break it three weeks later.

You are not paying for a tool. You are paying for it working, being safe, and someone accountable when it stops.

02 — What we install

One agent. Three jobs. Five days.

Not a demo, not a PDF. A running agent on hardware you own, reachable from the app you already open every morning, doing work you named before we started.

01

The hardened server

A private VPS in your own account. Firewall closed, SSH key only, no open ports, secrets in an env file, agent survives reboot, spend cap set on the key.

  • Hardened before anything runs
  • Version-pinned, updates on your schedule
  • You own the server and the data
02

Your agent, your channel

Reachable from Telegram (or WhatsApp where supported) with a linked account in your name — not a shared bot, not someone else's server.

  • One channel, one agent, one identity
  • Draft-first mode on anything that sends
  • Nothing leaves without your approval
03

Three workflows that run

You pick three jobs that repeat. We build them, test them live with you on a call, and write the handbook that explains each one in plain language.

  • Morning brief · inbox triage · follow-up nudges
  • Or pick your own three
  • Tested together, not promised
04

The handover & the guardrails

A 45-minute handover call, recorded. A one-page do-and-don't sheet. Fourteen days of fix-it support. We decline clients we cannot make safe.

  • Recorded handover + written handbook
  • 14 days of support after go-live
  • Cost sheet in your currency

03 — How it runs

Five days, in the open.

You are on the call, not waiting on a status email. Each day ends with something running that you can message.

Day 0

Intake call

45 minutes. What you actually do every day, which tools hold your data, which channel you already live in, and the three jobs worth automating. We tell you on the call if you are not a fit.

Day 1–2

Server & channel

Hardened VPS, your accounts, your identity. The agent boots on its own and survives a reboot. You message it the same day.

Day 3–4

Three workflows

We build the jobs you named, test each one against real inputs, and tighten them until they behave the way you expect.

Day 5

Handover call

Recorded walkthrough of everything, your handbook, your cost sheet, and fourteen days of fix-it support. You own all of it.

04 — Approval first

It drafts. You approve. It acts.

Anything that sends, posts, pays, deletes, or publishes runs in draft mode until you say go. Every approval is one tap. Every action is logged.

That is the difference between an agent you trust with a business and a demo you admire.
