Process

Five days you can see.

Every day ends with something running. You are on the calls, not waiting on status emails. If a day slips, you hear it that day.

Timeline
Day 0 → Day 5WebGL
Day 0

Intake call

45 minutes on a call. We map your week, your tools, your channel, and pick three jobs. You get an honest yes or no at the end of it.

Day 1

Server, hardened

VPS provisioned in your account, firewall closed, keys set, agent installed as a supervised service. Verified by reboot, not by assumption.

Day 2

Channel connected

Your channel account linked to your agent. You send it a message and get a reply from your own infrastructure.

Day 3

Workflow one

First job built and tested against real data. You approve the output format before it goes anywhere.

Day 4

Workflows two & three

Remaining jobs built, failure paths tested, and approval gating confirmed on anything that sends.

Day 5

Handover

Recorded walkthrough, handbook, safety sheet, cost sheet, root credentials handed over, and 14 days of support begins.

After day five

It keeps running. We keep answering.

The agent runs without you. For the first fortnight we are on the hook for anything that breaks.
