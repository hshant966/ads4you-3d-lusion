The four parts

What "done" means.

Each part below is a deliverable with a checkable end state — not a phase of a deck.

01 — The hardened server

Your VPS, closed by default

A private VPS in an account you own. Firewall with no open ports, SSH key auth only, secrets in an env file, agent installed as a service that comes back after a reboot, and a spend cap on the API key.

  • No open inbound ports
  • Key-only SSH, no password login
  • Agent runs as a supervised service
  • API spend capped so a loop cannot bill you
  • You get the root credentials
02 — Your agent, your channel

Reachable where you already are

A channel account in your own name, linked to the agent on your server. You message the agent the same way you message a colleague. No shared bot, no third-party holding your data.

  • Telegram first; WhatsApp where the agent supports it
  • Single identity, single agent
  • Draft-first on anything that sends
  • Every action written to a log you can read
03 — Three workflows

The jobs you actually repeat

You name three recurring jobs in the intake call. We build them, test each against real inputs, and fix the behaviour until it matches what you would have done.

  • Typical: morning brief, inbox triage, follow-up nudges
  • Or three you choose from your own week
  • Tested live on a call, not claimed in writing
  • Failed runs retry, and never double-send
04 — Handover & guardrails

You own it on day five

A recorded 45-minute handover of the whole system, a two-page written handbook, a one-page do-and-don't safety sheet, your own cost sheet, and fourteen days of fix-it support.

  • Recorded walkthrough of every workflow
  • Written handbook in plain language
  • Do-and-don't sheet for safe operation
  • 14 days of support after go-live

Boundaries

What we will not do.

We do not resell hosting, and we do not take a cut of your API spend. You pay the provider directly, at cost, with the spend cap we set together.

We do not build a "custom AI employee" for every request. If your need is outside one agent with three workflows, we will say so on the intake call — before you pay anything.
